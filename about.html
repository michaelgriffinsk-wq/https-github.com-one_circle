What is One Circle?

Welcome to One Circle. We are a collective of musicians, creatives, and worshippers dedicated to raising a sound that honors God. What started as a simple gathering has grown into a powerful movement of fellowship through music.

Through our live sessions and events, our goal is to create an atmosphere where people can encounter The Father's love, worship freely, and experience community.
